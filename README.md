# Planet FM: Earth is speaking, Are we listening?

**Planet FM** is an interactive application designed to make Earth-science information more accessible, engaging, and easier to understand by transforming location-based environmental data into meaningful visual and audio experiences.

The application allows users to select a geographical location and explore a variety of environmental topics, including air quality, greenhouse gases, natural disasters, water resources, energy, wildfires, biodiversity, sea-level change, and agriculture. By organising information into distinct categories, Planet FM enables users to explore environmental conditions relevant to specific locations.

One of the application's key features is **sonification**, the process of translating data into sound. Alongside visual representations such as graphs, Planet FM uses variations in audio to reflect changes in the underlying data. This allows users not only to observe environmental trends visually but also to experience them through listening. By combining sight and sound, the application offers an alternative approach to interpreting complex datasets and exploring environmental patterns.

Another important feature of Planet FM is its **user-interactive platform**, which encourages users to contribute their own perspectives and observations. Users can share reviews and upload images of specific locations, adding real-world context to the environmental information presented by the application. This participatory feature helps create a more engaging experience by connecting scientific data with people's experiences of the places around them.

Planet FM aims to bridge the gap between scientific information and public understanding. Rather than relying solely on numerical values, charts, and technical visualisations, it combines interactive exploration, auditory feedback, and user contributions to make environmental information more approachable.

The application is intended for students, educators, researchers, environmental enthusiasts, and the general public who wish to explore Earth's changing conditions. It encourages curiosity, supports environmental awareness, and provides opportunities for users to engage with information and contribute their observations.

Ultimately, **Planet FM transforms environmental data from something we simply observe into something we can experience, hear, and explore together.** By combining visualisation, sonification, and user participation, the application seeks to foster a deeper understanding of our planet and the changes shaping its future.
